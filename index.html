About Us

A family kitchen that grew into a roadside favourite

I grew up watching my father cook for big family gatherings, and he always said that good gravy cannot be hurried. Around 2021 I took that lesson and opened YG King on Itarsi Road, right beside Sanjivani School.

We started with a few chicken dishes and a small group of regulars. Over the years people kept asking for mutton, then biryani, then paneer for the vegetarians in the family, so the menu grew one request at a time.

Today you will find students' parents, travellers on the highway and neighbours who walk in every week. If you eat here once, I hope you will come back with your family.

  • Fresh chicken and mutton, bought and cooked the same day
  • Half and full plates, so you pay only for what you eat
  • Dine-in seating with an open kitchen you can see
  • Easy to find: Tikrani Naka, beside Sanjivani School
  • Order by call or WhatsApp, ready when you arrive
